Privacy note · 11 November 2026

Your game and your information

This educational game is a project by Sam O’Brien-Olinger, Sam Tim Solutions, Ireland. For privacy questions, correction or removal requests, contact samobrienolinger@gmail.com.

Nickname and saved game

The game saves the nickname you choose, your choices and progress in this browser so you can continue a game. Use a fictional nickname, not your full name, contact details or sensitive information. “Start a new game” clears the existing saved game. Browser site-data controls can also remove it, but clearing the entire GitHub Pages origin may affect other projects hosted there. A shared-device user may be able to see the saved game.

This note does not introduce any account, score-upload service or tracking identifier. Saved game information is retained locally until cleared or replaced; no automatic retention deadline is promised by the current game.

Videos and external links

The resource videos are provided as ordinary links instead of embedded players. No YouTube player or thumbnail needs to load to display those links. Opening a video leaves this site; YouTube then receives connection information such as your IP address and applies its own privacy and cookie choices. Source and developer links similarly lead to separate websites.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages delivers the site and processes technical requests, including connection information such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. Its records are separate from the game's local save. See GitHub's privacy statement for its retention and international processing. The game cannot erase records held independently by a hosting provider.

Contact and your rights

Email you choose to send includes your email address and message. It is used to answer the enquiry and manage any related issue, based on the legitimate interest in responding to people who contact the project. Please send only what is needed. Retention depends on resolving the matter and any applicable legal requirements; ask about a specific record rather than assuming an automatic deletion period.

For personal information processed by the project, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to legitimate-interest processing. Rights have legal conditions and exceptions. Contact the email above; the usual GDPR response period is one month, subject to permitted extensions. You can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.
